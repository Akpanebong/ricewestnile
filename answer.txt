Answers about RICE West Nile digital systems

1. Distribution of items to communities and beneficiary verification

System name: M&E (Monitoring and Evaluation) System, specifically the Indicators and Data Entry module, linked to Projects and Locations. The system records project and donor, indicator, year/month, district/sub-county/settlement, sex, national/refugee status, disability disaggregation, enterprise type, groups reached, group members, and male/female totals. It provides dashboards, charts, CSV import, and Excel/PDF exports.

Advantage: It gives RICE West Nile one central, reportable record of results and beneficiary reach, with useful disaggregation by location, sex, refugee/national status, disability, project, and year.

Disadvantage: The current implementation is mainly an aggregate M&E data system. It does not contain a dedicated individual beneficiary registry with unique beneficiary IDs, duplicate checking, identity verification, distribution-item/stock records, or beneficiary signatures. Therefore, it can report reach but cannot by itself prove that a particular person received a particular item.

2. Donor proposals, pledges, reports, and financial tracking

System name: Project, Finance and Donor Reporting functionality, supported by the Communication reporting module and GARCIS donor-compliance features. Projects store donor and project information; Finance tracks donor-coded cash requisitions, accounting forms, expenditure, balances, and approvals; Communication stores presentations and periodic reports with review status and comments; GARCIS supports compliance and donor-reporting preparation.

Advantage: Donor-related project, budget, expenditure, accounting, and reporting information can be connected across operational workflows and supported by approval records, review status, and downloadable reports.

Disadvantage: There is no separate donor CRM or grants-management module in the current code for a complete proposal and pledge pipeline. In particular, dedicated proposal records, pledge commitments, donor correspondence, renewal reminders, and pledge-to-income reconciliation are not modeled. Donor tracking is therefore spread across Project, Finance, Communication, and compliance records rather than managed in one donor portal.

3. Community feedback and complaints

System name: GARCIS Governance – Stakeholder Engagement module.

It records the stakeholder, source area, channel (feedback form, survey, dialogue, or community meeting), subject, feedback, assignee, follow-up date, and status (received, reviewed, actioned, or closed).

Advantage: It creates a clear follow-up trail from receiving community feedback to assigning responsibility and closing the issue, while also supporting different community-engagement channels.

Disadvantage: It is primarily an internal record-management tool. The current implementation does not show a public/anonymous intake portal, complaint category and severity, confidential case handling, SLA/escalation rules, a dedicated response field, or automated complainant notifications. Those features would be needed for a stronger complaints-response mechanism.

4. Human resources

System name: HR Information System (HRIS), available through the HR, Recruitment, Performance, Orientation, and Exit workflows.

It manages employee profiles and related personal information, recruitment requests and applicants, attendance (including location information), leave applications and multi-level approval, training, appraisals, performance-improvement plans, orientation, forums, situation reports, and exit processes.

Advantage: It supports a broad employee lifecycle in one system, with role-based approvals, leave balances and reports, attendance exports, recruitment stages, performance reviews, training records, and onboarding/orientation workflows.

Disadvantage: The reviewed functionality does not include a full payroll, benefits, tax, pension, or comprehensive workforce-planning module. HR records are also distributed across several submodules, so consistent data maintenance and permissions are important.

5. Procurement

System name: Procurement Management System.

It covers procurement plans, products, suppliers, budgets, requisitions, approval workflows, requests for quotations (RFQs), supplier communication, purchase orders, contracts, and supplier/spend reports. Procurement plans are linked to projects, donors, fiscal years, and project budgets.

Advantage: It provides an auditable procure-to-order workflow with supplier records, quotation/RFQ handling, multi-stage approvals, budget-limit validation, purchase-order tracking, and expenditure/supplier reporting.

Disadvantage: Procurement accuracy depends on correct project budgets, supplier data, and approvals being maintained. The current module does not appear to be a complete warehouse, stock, goods-received, or community-distribution system; post-purchase asset and inventory tracking is handled separately by the Assets module.

Overall conclusion: The platform is an integrated enterprise system made up of M&E, Finance, Communication, GARCIS, HR, Procurement, Assets, and related Project/Account modules. It is strong for internal workflow, approvals, monitoring, reporting, and auditability. The main gaps relevant to these questions are individual beneficiary verification/distribution tracking and a full donor proposal/pledge-management function.
