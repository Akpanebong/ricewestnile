Indicator Guidance
What makes a good indicator?

A good indicator provides reliable evidence that can be used to measure change, progress, performance, or achievement of an expected result.

Use standardized and consistent terminology across the M&E framework.

Select a unit of measure that can be consistently collected and reported.

Make the indicator specific enough to distinguish success from failure.

Ensure the required data can realistically be collected and verified.

Maintain a direct relationship with the parent output.
